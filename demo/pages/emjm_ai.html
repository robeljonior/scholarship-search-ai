Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Artificial Intelligence (demo)

A two-year Master of Science programme delivered by three universities in Belgium, France and Germany.

The programme is fully funded for selected students: the scholarship covers full tuition, travel and a monthly allowance of EUR 1,400.

Eligibility

Open to applicants of all nationalities holding a bachelor's degree in computer science, mathematics or engineering.

Minimum GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0. English: IELTS overall 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 90.

Documents: transcript, diploma, CV, motivation letter, two letters of recommendation, passport.

Deadline

Application deadline: 15 January 2027, 23:59 CET.
