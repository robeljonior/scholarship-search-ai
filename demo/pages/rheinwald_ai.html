Rheinwald University Excellence Scholarship - MSc Artificial Intelligence (demo)

Rheinwald University, Germany. The scholarship is fully funded: full tuition waiver and a monthly stipend of EUR 992.

Open to applicants from all nationalities with a bachelor's degree in computer science or related fields. Minimum GPA of 2.8 out of 4.0. IELTS 6.5.

At least 1 year of professional work experience.

Deadline: 31 December 2026.
